Who Has the BEST High School Marching Band in Ocean County? Vote Now!

There is just something about a Friday night football game in Ocean County. The lights are on, the crowd is cheering, the football players are on the field and the marching band is right there in the bleachers playing their hearts out.

92.7 WOBM

That is school spirit!

I have always loved sitting near the marching band. In fact, when I was younger, I tried to play the clarinet, flute and drums. Let's just say I didn't exactly become a marching-band sensation!

Ocean County Has Some Great Bands

Now it's time for a little friendly competition!

We want to know which Ocean County high school marching band YOU think deserves some extra attention. Maybe it's your hometown school. Maybe your son or daughter is a student there. Maybe you graduated from that school and still proudly wear the colors.

Or maybe you simply heard the band play at a football game, parade or competition and thought, WOW, they are GOOD!

Here are the Ocean County public high schools we're putting into the conversation:

  • Barnegat High School
  • Brick Memorial High School
  • Brick Township High School
  • Central Regional High School
  • Jackson Township High School
  • Lacey Township High School
  • Lakewood High School
  • Manchester Township High School
  • New Egypt High School
  • Pinelands Regional High School
  • Point Pleasant Beach High School
  • Point Pleasant Borough High School
  • Southern Regional High School
  • Toms River High School East
  • Toms River High School North
  • Toms River High School South

Who Gets Your Vote?

This isn't about being too serious. It's about having fun, showing some school pride and giving these talented students some well-deserved love!

Head over to the 92.7 WOBM Facebook Page and vote for your favorite marching band.

And if you don't see your favorite listed, tell us in the comments!

We're going to watch those votes come in and then narrow the field down to our TOP 5.

Keep Reading: Toms River's Rockin' Fall Festival with Pumpkins, Music and More!

So, Ocean County, grab your school colors, cheer for your hometown and make some noise for the marching bands!

Who has your vote?  Let's have some fun with this one!
