Manchester United fans demand intervention over Bruno Fernandes injury situation

Jonny Maze
Tue 29 September 2026 18:00, UK
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 0:45
Instaread

Manchester United supporters have been voicing concern and frustration over the handling of Bruno Fernandes during the current international break.

Bruno Fernandes has been playing injured for more than three weeks, according to ESPN, arriving for Portuguese duty with the issue, and in recent days, this situation has sparked a hot debate within the fanbase.

Despite this, Fernandes played against Wales last week, with Portugal boss Jorge Jesus stating Fernandes had been playing through pain. He missed training and wasn’t in the squad for Portugal against Norway, but despite clear problems, he has remained with his national team, not returning to Manchester United to be looked after.

This situation has now led to fans demanding that Fernandes be immediately returned to Carrington for treatment, to avoid a worse situation.

Fans demand Manchester United action over Bruno Fernandes

Fernandes staying with the Portugal squad if he is injured makes absolutely no sense and will only see his poor campaign continue if he keeps playing through injury. On TalkingPoints, one supporter, @knightinwhitesatin, admitted that “it’s reminiscent of [Luke] Shaw going on international duty when he was injured” with United paying for this, with Shaw having a long-term absence.

What makes the Fernandes situation even more concerning is how United moved quickly to see Patrick Dorgu return after his injury for Denmark. User @chillout on TalkingPoints asked the all-important question, “why haven’t United made a bigger fuss over this?”, which is the main concern surrounding the Fernandes situation, with a handful of potential reasons.

Of course, even before international duty, Michael Carrick continued playing Fernandes through injury, but as @seanfisher, points out, “there would be outrage if he comes back to Manchester with a serious injury”, and the real answer to the above question is United should be making a bigger fuss.

News of an injury to Fernandes is a worry, despite so few details being disclosed about the seriousness of his problem. This is because he is a player who doesn’t pick up injuries and wouldn’t miss a game unless he was in serious pain, having played countless times injured for United over the years.

But this just highlights how United and Portugal are both guilty of not protecting the 32-year-old by not resting or treating his injury as serious. But as @dancoombs points out, “Bruno is his own worst enemy at times as he will be insisting on playing through it”. This point was furthered by @seanfisher, who stated “United should step in”, to save Fernandes from himself, as he won’t stop himself playing until it’s too late.

Portugal and United should absolutely be discussing this and doing what is best for Fernandes, which would be a long rest, treatment for his pain, and active steps to extend his career. So if he is sent back to Carrington, then Carrick needs to also protect him, not play him through injury and pain for short-term gain.

He isn’t as young as he once was, and therefore Fernandes needs to be given rest, not played 90 minutes against Sabah, rotated with substitutes, and other methods of keeping him fit and fresh. But that all starts with Portugal sending Fernandes home and not persisting with him in the camp.
