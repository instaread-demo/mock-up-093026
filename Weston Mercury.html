Hundreds of people lit up Weston-super-Mare's seafront as they took part in Weston Hospicecare's annual Moonlight Beach Walk.

Participants completed either a 5km or 10km route, setting off from the Grand Pier before making their way along the promenade.

Many walkers wore brightly coloured accessories, glow sticks and fancy dress as they raised money for the hospice, which provides free care and support to people with life-limiting illnesses across Weston-super-Mare and the surrounding area.

The event is one of Weston Hospicecare's biggest fundraisers and also serves as an opportunity for people to remember loved ones while supporting the charity.

Walkers were welcomed back to the Grand Pier for an after-party featuring music, entertainment and refreshments after completing the challenge.

The annual event also offered participants the chance to remember loved ones while supporting the charity.

Among those taking part were families, friends, hospice supporters and volunteers, all helping to raise vital funds for the charity's services.
